Questions people ask before choosing ADR.

These short guides explain how different processes work and what parties can expect.

Mediation

What is Alternative Dispute Resolution?

ADR is a group of processes that resolve disputes outside traditional court proceedings, including mediation, arbitration and conciliation.

Choosing a process

Mediation or arbitration: what is the difference?

Mediation helps parties shape their own agreement. Arbitration asks an independent arbitrator to make a formal determination.

Early resolution

Can debt disputes be resolved through mediation?

Suitable payment, repayment and commercial disagreements may benefit from structured dialogue before positions harden.

Arbitration

Understanding arbitration agreements

An arbitration agreement identifies the parties’ decision to refer disputes to arbitration and can shape the procedure used.

Decision-making

Alternatives to court litigation

ADR can offer privacy, flexibility and a process tailored to the issues, parties and desired outcome.
