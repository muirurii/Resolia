Mediation

Finding a way forward.

A structured and confidential process that gives parties an opportunity to be heard, understand each other’s concerns and shape their own solution.

Better conversations. Brighter outcomes.

How mediation works

A guided process with room for every voice.

  1. Initial enquiry

    The parties or their advisers contact Resolia Conseil with basic information about the matter.

  2. Engagement of parties

    The process, expectations and mediator’s role are explained clearly.

  3. Preparation

    The parties identify the issues, participants and relevant information.

  4. Mediation session

    The mediator facilitates a confidential and constructive discussion.

  5. Resolution

    When agreement is reached, the agreed terms can be documented clearly.

Mediation may be suitable for:

  • Commercial disputes
  • Business partnerships
  • Workplace and employment matters
  • Property and construction disputes
  • Family and relationship disputes
  • Neighbour and community matters
  • Other civil and commercial disagreements
