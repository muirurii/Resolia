Arbitration

A structured alternative.

A private dispute resolution process in which parties submit a matter to an independent arbitrator for determination.

Clarity today. Certainty tomorrow.

The arbitration process

Professional, impartial and clearly managed.

  1. Dispute arises

    The parties identify a matter requiring formal determination.

  2. Arbitration agreement

    The arbitration clause or agreement is reviewed and the procedure confirmed.

  3. Appointment of arbitrator

    A qualified and impartial arbitrator is appointed.

  4. Exchange of positions

    The parties provide their statements, documents and relevant evidence.

  5. Hearing or determination

    The matter is considered under the agreed procedure.

  6. Arbitral award

    The arbitrator issues a formal determination of the dispute.

Arbitration may be suitable for:

  • Commercial disputes
  • Contractual disputes
  • Construction disputes
  • Partnership disputes
  • Shareholder disputes
  • Professional services disputes
  • Cross-border disagreements
  • Other complex disputes
