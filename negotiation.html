Negotiation facilitation

Structured dialogue for important decisions.

Professional support for negotiations where communication, clarity and a workable agreement matter.

Clear interests. Constructive discussion. Practical agreement.

Our approach

Moving from positions to practical interests.

  1. Define the objective

    We clarify the issues to be negotiated, the decision-makers and the desired outcome.

  2. Prepare the process

    We agree the structure, agenda, information needs and ground rules.

  3. Facilitate dialogue

    We keep communication focused, balanced and productive.

  4. Explore options

    Parties consider alternatives that respond to their underlying needs and interests.

  5. Record agreement

    The agreed terms and responsibilities are set out with clarity.

We facilitate negotiations involving:

  • Commercial agreements
  • Business partnerships
  • Workplace matters
  • Contract discussions
  • Property and project matters
  • Other situations requiring effective communication
