Who we are

Conflict can be handled without unnecessary confrontation.

Resolia Conseil Ltd is an Alternative Dispute Resolution firm providing professional mediation, arbitration, conciliation and facilitated negotiation.

Our vision

To make peaceful resolution the first choice in every conflict.

Our mission

What guides us

Integrity in process. Dignity for every person.

Behind every dispute are people, relationships, businesses and interests that matter. We listen carefully, remain impartial and focus on solutions that can work in practice.

A good resolution should not only bring an end to a dispute. It should create a better way forward.
