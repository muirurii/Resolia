Behind the vision of Resolia Conseil Ltd is a seasoned practitioner with more than 30 years of senior management, negotiation and practical dispute-resolution experience. As Founder and Senior Advisor, he brings depth, wisdom and steady judgement to the firm, helping parties find calm and constructive ways forward.

His path to mediation grew out of decades in business and project management, where he coordinated major projects, managed supplier relationships and resolved workplace and interpersonal issues. Since 2012 he has worked as an independent consultant in conflict management, facilitating dialogue and negotiated settlements. He is an Associate Member of the Chartered Institute of Arbitrators (ACIArb), has completed Ciarb’s International Introduction to ADR, and is a Certified Professional Mediator.

Through Resolia Conseil, he works mainly with businesses and organisations on commercial disputes, partnership and shareholder disagreements, debt, creditor and restructuring matters, and labour and workplace disputes. He also supports families and communities in land and property, succession and family estate matters, often working alongside advocates, professional advisers and local authorities.

He believes that good resolution begins with careful listening. His approach is warm yet firmly neutral, grounded in independence, impartiality, confidentiality and procedural fairness, with a focus on each party’s real interests and on practical solutions that preserve relationships. He speaks English, Kiswahili, Gujarati, Hindi and Punjabi, so many parties can be heard in the language they know best.

Behind Resolia Conseil Ltd is a lawyer and certified mediator who believes that every dispute is a conversation in waiting. As Co-Founder and Director, she leads the firm’s mediation and ADR practice, with a clear commitment to helping people move forward with dignity and confidence.

She holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) and is a Certified Professional Mediator. Her path to mediation grew naturally out of her legal work: researching civil and commercial disputes at a law firm showed her how much parties gain when they choose dialogue early. Today she brings that understanding, together with strong skills in negotiation, settlement facilitation and drafting agreements, to every matter. She has also completed CIPIT’s Data Protection Course, reflecting her commitment to confidentiality.

Through Resolia Conseil, she works mainly with companies and organisations on corporate and commercial disputes, debt recovery, business and partnership disagreements, and workplace conflicts, helping them reach resolution efficiently, privately and with important relationships intact. She also walks alongside families and individuals in personal matters, with the same care and discretion.

For her, every mediation begins with building rapport. She creates a calm, respectful space where each party feels safe, heard and taken seriously. Her approach is warm yet firmly neutral, grounded in impartiality, confidentiality, fairness and practical problem-solving. Once the parties find common ground, she helps them capture it in clear, well-drafted settlement agreements that last. Sessions are available in person and virtually.
